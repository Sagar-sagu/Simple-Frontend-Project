What is State Scholarship Portal?

The State Scholarship Portal (SSP) is the official unified online platform
run by the Government of Karnataka to manage and disburse
both pre-matric and post-matric scholarships.
Managed by the Center for e-Governance,
it integrates directly with welfare department databases to
automatically credit funds into students' bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
